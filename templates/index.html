About Us

Serving Eastern Samar through science, technology, and innovation

DOST PSTO Eastern Samar brings national science and technology programs closer to local communities, schools, enterprises, and public institutions across the province.

The office supports technology adoption, scholarship information, community-based projects, and innovation services that help Eastern Samar build practical solutions for local needs.
